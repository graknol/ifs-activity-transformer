About the Model

This application uses RoBERTa BigBird, a state-of-the-art transformer model designed for long sequences. The model is fine-tuned on your activity mapping data to learn patterns and predict appropriate activity paths for new activities.

Training Tips:

  • Ensure your data has consistent labels and good coverage of all activity types
  • More training data generally leads to better performance
  • Training may take 15-30 minutes depending on data size and hardware
  • The model will automatically save checkpoints during training
